# 🚀 Start Here — Your AI Learning Roadmap

Welcome! This roadmap is designed for **absolute beginners** to **intermediate learners** who want to go from "I've never used AI" to "I can build AI-powered solutions" — step by step, with achievable goals.

> **Your background doesn't matter.** Whether you're a complete beginner, a Python developer, or someone looking to add AI to their workflow — there's a path for you.

---

## Choose Your Starting Point

### Path A: The Everyday Explorer (No Coding Required)
**Best for:** Non-programmers, business professionals, creators, entrepreneurs
**Timeframe:** 2-3 months to practical results
**Focus:** Using AI tools, prompt engineering, no-code automation

| Step | Topic | What You'll Learn | Duration |
|------|-------|-------------------|----------|
| 1 | **AI Basics** | What AI/LLMs are, how to use ChatGPT/Claude for daily tasks | 1 week |
| 2 | **Prompt Mastery** | Aim/Context/Rules framework, role prompting, prompt patterns | 1-2 weeks |
| 3 | **Creative Tools** | Image generation (MidJourney, DALL-E), audio (Eleven Labs), video AI | 1 week |
| 4 | **No-Code Automation** | Build workflows with n8n, connect AI to real tools | 1-2 weeks |
| 5 | **AI Agents** | Understand agentic AI, build simple AI assistants | 1 week |

**Start here:** [AI Consumer Tools Overview](../2-ai-consumer-tools/ai-tools/meta-harness.md)

### Path B: The Python Developer (Coding-Focused)
**Best for:** Developers who know Python basics and want to add AI skills
**Timeframe:** 5-6 months part-time
**Focus:** Building AI models, local LLMs, production deployments

| Step | Topic | What You'll Learn | Duration |
|------|-------|-------------------|----------|
| 0 | **Setup & Clean Code** | Poetry, pre-commit, type hints, CI basics | 1 week |
| 1 | **Math Foundations** | Linear algebra, probability, gradient descent intuition | 2-3 weeks |
| 2 | **Python ML Stack** | NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, scikit-learn | 3 weeks |
| 3 | **Deep Learning from Scratch** | Neural networks in NumPy, back-propagation | 2 weeks |
| 4 | **PyTorch Mastery** | Tensors, training loops, CNNs, GPU basics | 3 weeks |
| 5 | **Modern LLMs** | Transformers, Hugging Face, prompt engineering, RAG | 3 weeks |
| 6 | **Production & MLOps** | Docker, FastAPI, CI/CD, model serving | 3 weeks |
| 7 | **Specialization** | Pick CV, NLP, Tabular, RL, or Edge AI | 8-12 weeks |

**Start here:** [AI-Python Learning Path](../ai-learning-plans/ai-python-learning-path.md)

### Path C: The AI Enthusiast (Local & Private AI)
**Best for:** Privacy-conscious users, tinkerers, people with limited internet
**Timeframe:** 1-2 weeks to get started
**Focus:** Running AI models locally, free and open-source

| Step | Topic | What You'll Learn | Duration |
|------|-------|-------------------|----------|
| 1 | **Local AI Basics** | Why local AI, choosing your first model | 1 day |
| 2 | **Ollama Setup** | Install Ollama, run your first local model | 1 hour |
| 3 | **LM Studio** | Visual model management, performance monitoring | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | **Hardware Setup** | Understand RAM, VRAM, GPU requirements | 1 day |
| 5 | **Fine-Tuning** | LoRA, custom datasets, model quantization | 1-2 weeks |

**Start here:** [Beginners Guide to Local AI](../5-local-ai/getting-started/beginners-guide-to-local-ai.md)

## Quick Reference: The 70/30 Rule

| Your Level | 70% Should Be | 30% Should Be |
|------------|---------------|---------------|
| **Beginner** | Open-source tools (free, self-built) | Proprietary tools (for comparison) |
| **Intermediate** | Open tools for innovation | Proprietary tools for scale |
| **Advanced** | Open-source for research | Proprietary for production |

## Key Principles

1. **Start with consumption, move to creation** — Use AI tools first, then learn to build them
2. **Prompt engineering is the core skill** — Learn to communicate with AI effectively
3. **Build real things** — Every phase should have a practical project
4. **Learn in public** — Share your progress, teach others, join the community
5. **Open first, proprietary next** — Master open tools before relying on paid ones
6. **Consistency beats intensity** — 30 minutes daily > 5 hours once a week
