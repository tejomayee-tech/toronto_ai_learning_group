    Get Started with Local, Private and Secure community free AI Models

    Why you should try Local AI models?

    🛡️ Privacy & Security

    • Total Privacy: Your data never leaves your PC; no tech giants see or store your prompts.
    • No Training: Your chats aren't used for training.
    • Device Security: Your files stay protected by your own Operating System security and encryption.

    🏠 Local Control

    • 100% Offline: Works without Wi-Fi or in "Airplane Mode."
    • Ownership: You own the model files forever—no censorship or service shutdowns.
    • Air-Gap Ready: Can run on PCs physically disconnected from the internet for max security.

    💰 Savings & Compliance

    • Zero Cost: No subscriptions or per-message fees.
    • Easy Compliance: Simplifies legal privacy rules since data stays local.
    • No Limits: Download and use models without hitting usage caps.

    1. Ollama (The Official Chat App)

    Ollama has evolved from a hidden "engine" into a full desktop app with its own chat interface.

    • Step 1: Download & Install: Go to ollama.com and download the Windows installer. Run the .exe file.
    • Step 2: Open the App: Once installed, launch "Ollama" from your Start Menu. A clean chat window will appear.
    • Step 3: Pick Your Model: In the chat window, click the Model Selection dropdown at the top.
    • Step 4: Search for Gemma: Type "Gemma 3" in the search box within the app.

    • Recommendation: Choose Gemma 3 4B to start with. Bigger models need higher RAM capacity

    • Step 5: Automatic Pull: Click Download. The app will show a progress bar. Once it hits 100%, the chat box will activate.

    • Step 6: Chat: Just start typing.

    2. LM Studio (The Visual Designer)

    LM Studio is for users who want to see exactly how their computer is performing. It feels more like a professional "studio" for AI.

    • Step 1: Install: Download from lmstudio.ai. Run the installer and open the app.
    • Step 2: Find Gemma: Click the Magnifying Glass icon on the left sidebar. In the search bar at the top, type "Gemma 3".
    • Step 3: Choose the "Safe" Version: You will see a list of results. Look for the version that has a Green Checkmark or says "Likely to Fit." * Look for a file ending in "Q4_K_M" or "Q6_K". Click the Download button on the right.
    • Step 4: Load to RAM: Click the Speech Bubble (Chat) icon on the left. Click the "Select a model to load" bar at the top and select the Gemma file you just downloaded.
    • Step 5: Watch the Meters: On the right side of the screen, LM Studio shows you exactly how much of your RAM and GPU memory is being used.

    3. GPT4All (The Simple Assistant)

    GPT4All is built for speed and privacy. It is the best choice if you have a laptop without a dedicated graphics card.

    • Step 1: Install: Download from gpt4all.io. Follow the installation wizard and open the app.
    • Step 2: Browse Models: Click the Models icon (it looks like a cloud or a list).
    • Step 3: One-Click Download: Find Gemma 3 in the list. Unlike LM Studio, you don't have to pick a file type—GPT4All has already selected the best version for general PCs. Just click Download.
    • Step 4: Chatting: Click the Chat icon. Select Gemma from the dropdown at the top, and you’re ready to go.
