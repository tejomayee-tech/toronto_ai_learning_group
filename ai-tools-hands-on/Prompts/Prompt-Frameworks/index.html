    Prompt Frameworks

    Markdown Table – Prompt‑Framework Overview

    Framework Expanded Acronym Purpose & Use Cases Key Values & Details
    PAIR "Purpose, Audience, Instructions, Resources" General Purpose: Best for creating content, emails, or reports where the who and why are critical. Focuses on the intent and the target reader to ensure the tone is appropriate.
    FABRIC "Format, Audience, Background, Role, Instructions, Contexts" Complex Projects: Ideal for deep research, technical writing, or strategic planning. Emphasizes Context and Background to prevent the AI from making unwarranted assumptions.
    APRICOT "Audience, Purpose, Role, Instructions, Context, Output, Tone" Creative & Marketing: Great for brand‑voice consistency and specific content types (blogs, ads). Highlights Tone and Output, ensuring the final result sounds right and fits a specific format.
    CRISPE "Context, Role, Instruction, Scheme, Params, Evaluate" Technical/Data Tasks: Best for coding, data analysis, or structured outputs like JSON/Markdown. Unique for its Scheme (structure) and Evaluate (asking the AI to check its own work).
    CO‑STAR "Context, Objective, Style, Tone, Audience, Response" Business Communication: Highly effective for professional tasks and multi‑step reasoning. Focuses on the Objective (the “what”) and the Response format to guarantee utility.
    CREATE "Character, Request, Examples, Adjustments, Type, Extras" Iterative Writing: Useful for refining drafts or generating creative stories. Strong emphasis on Examples (few‑shot prompting) to guide the AI by style.
    ERA "Expectation, Role, Action" Quick Tasks: Perfect for short, rapid‑fire prompts where you need speed over depth. Simple and minimalist; concentrates on Action to get immediate results.
